About

Daisy Furrokh is a painter, saxophonist and scientist based in Zürich. She is currently studying for a doctorate in Tropical Molecular Ecology, researching pollination and forest use in South India. The anthropological aspects of this work are a great inspiration in her paintings. She wasn't sure whether to write this part in third person or not.
